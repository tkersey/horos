## Cross-cutting paper observations

- **Values and abstraction limits:** `coalescing_witness_tests.zig`, “coalescing raw validator rejects separately admissible wrong output”, and `capture_summary_tests.zig`, “summary checker rejects a well-typed OR substituted for XOR”, reject type-correct value changes. Matching interaction summaries cannot replace these checkers.
- **Order and labeled replies:** forwarding already preserves two operations after parameter coalescing. `evidence_forwarding.observeInteractions` now observes the actual validated candidate; the source service interaction test and independent source/native trace membership reject the swapped-reply mapping despite its equal final state.
- **Authority:** `evidence_forwarding_tests.zig`, “two installations of one handler descriptor do not establish equal evidence”, and `coalescing_witness_tests.zig`, “coalescing witness domains reject local nonbijections and authority merges”. Descriptor equality does not prove attachment equality.
- **Multiplicity:** `evidence_forwarding_tests.zig`, “actual duplicate capability arguments remove forwarding, not operations”, and `tail_duplication_tests.zig`, “copying an effectful tail must not execute its mutation twice”.
- **Exceptions:** `sequence_fusion_tests.zig`, “failing mapper and folder retain eager phase ordering”, and `capture_reduction_tests.zig`, “removed capture evaluation retains its arithmetic failure”. The service source/native trace scenarios additionally check cleanup on overflow and cancellation.
- **Progress:** `unfold_fusion_tests.zig`, “admissible wrong captured state and successor hoisting are rejected independently”, `leaf_inlining_tests.zig`, “non-leaf recursion is retained without unfolding”, and `loop_motion_tests.zig`, “an infinite loop with public observations remains untouched”. Effect-free does not imply terminating.
- **Edges:** `contification_tests.zig`, “admissible wrong incoming argument and swapped-edge mutations reject”, and `affine_parallel_tests.zig`, “independent acceptance rejects a well-typed wrong parallel state transfer”, preserve simultaneous predecessor reads.
- **Freshness:** `application_specialization_tests.zig`, “facts cannot be reused after constructor catalog mutation”, and `call_patterns_tests.zig`, “call-pattern checker rejects a well-typed wrong target and stale epoch”. New monitor/projection queries must obey the same immutable-subject boundary.

The new interaction consumer is advisory alongside the stronger local rule.
Inconclusive abstraction does not veto that rule; a demonstrated contradiction
must be resolved. These are scoped replacement contracts, not whole-program
termination, external exactly-once settlement, or unconditional completion claims.
